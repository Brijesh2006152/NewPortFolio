Here are a few modern, high-contrast dark theme palettes tailored specifically for your portfolio's layout.

Since your current design relies on distinct content blocks (the introduction banner, the skills cards, etc.), these palettes use a **deep background** combined with **brighter, distinct card backgrounds** and vibrant accent colors to make your text pop and ensure excellent readability.

---

## Theme 1: Cyber Punk / Tech Minimal (Highly Modern)

This theme uses a near-black background with a crisp teal accent, giving it a very clean, professional developer vibe.

* **Main Background:** `#0B0F19` (Deep Midnight Blue/Black)
* **Card/Section Backgrounds:** `#161B26` (Dark Slate Blue)
* **Primary Text:** `#F3F4F6` (Cool White)
* **Secondary/Muted Text:** `#9CA3AF` (Light Gray)
* **Accent Color (Headings/Buttons):** `#00F5D4` or `#38BDF8` (Bright Teal/Cyan)

## Theme 2: Nord Dark (Elegant & Soft on the Eyes)

If you want something dark but less harsh than pure black, the Nord palette offers incredible contrast using cool, icy tones.

* **Main Background:** `#2E3440` (Nord Polar Night)
* **Card/Section Backgrounds:** `#3B4252` (Lighter Polar Night)
* **Primary Text:** `#ECEFF4` (Snow Storm White)
* **Secondary/Muted Text:** `#D8DEE9` (Soft Gray-Blue)
* **Accent Color (Headings/Buttons):** `#88C0D0` (Frost Blue) or `#A3BE8C` (Sage Green)

## Theme 3: Deep Obsidian & Gold (Premium & Bold)

This palette gives off a sleek, high-end portfolio look. The warm gold/yellow accents stand out sharply against the dark obsidian base.

* **Main Background:** `#121212` (Pure Obsidian Black)
* **Card/Section Backgrounds:** `#1E1E1E` (Dark Charcoal)
* **Primary Text:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
* **Secondary/Muted Text:** `#A0A0A0` (Medium Gray)
* **Accent Color (Headings/Buttons):** `#FFB703` or `#F4A261` (Vibrant Amber/Gold)

---

## 💡 Quick CSS Implementation Tips for Your Layout

To make these look great on your specific React structure:

1. **Ditch the full opacity white borders:** Instead of bright white borders, use a very subtle border on your cards to give them depth against the dark background. For example: `border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05);`
2. **Keep the container separation:** Your current layout uses nice rounded corners and margins for the boxes (Web Development, Python, Java). Keep that structure, but just swap the light blue/green backgrounds out for the **Card Background** hex codes listed above.
3. **Text Contrast:** Ensure your smaller text (like the descriptions inside the skills cards) uses the **Secondary Text** color rather than pure white, which keeps it readable without causing eye strain.
